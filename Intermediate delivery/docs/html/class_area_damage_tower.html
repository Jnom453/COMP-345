Detailed Description

Area of effect tower: a shot damages every critter in a group.

Starting stats (level 1): cost 150, refund 75, range 2.5, power 12, rate of fire 0.7. It costs more and hits harder in total than the direct damage tower because it can hit several critters per shot.
