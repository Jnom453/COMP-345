Detailed Description

An enemy unit of the game.

Game rules:

  • A critter has a level, hit points, strength, speed and reward.
  • It loses hit points when a tower hits it, and dies when they reach 0. A critter that is already dead takes no more damage.
  • Killing a critter earns the player its reward in coins.
  • A critter that reaches the exit steals coins from the player, equal to its strength.
  • A slow effect multiplies its speed by a factor between 0 and 1.

The position is a progress value along the path: 0 at the entry, and 100 once the critter has reached the exit.

Critter is an Observable: it notifies its observers when it takes damage, is slowed, moves, or reaches the exit (see CritterObserver).
