Detailed Description

Base class of every tower in the game.

A tower has a buying cost, a refund value, a range, a power (damage per hit) and a rate of fire. Subclasses (DirectDamageTower, AreaDamageTower, SlowTower) differ in how they attack, by overriding attack().

Two design patterns are built around this class:

  • Strategy: the tower holds a TargetingStrategy that chooses which critter in range to shoot (see setStrategy() and chooseTarget()).
  • Decorator: TowerDecorator and its subclasses wrap a Tower to change its level, statistics or attack effects. For this reason all the getters and the attack functions are virtual: a decorator answers in place of the tower it wraps.

Game rules:

  • A tower starts at level 1. The maximum level is 3.
  • Upgrading costs buyingCost x current level coins. Each upgrade adds 5 power and 1.0 range. The rate of fire does not change.
  • An upgrade is refused, and nothing changes, if the tower is already at the maximum level or the player cannot afford it.

Tower is an Observable: it notifies its observers after a successful upgrade (see TowerObserver).
