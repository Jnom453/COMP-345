Detailed Description

The rules and the game loop of the playable Tower Defense game.

Game owns the Map, the Player, the towers the player placed and the current wave of critters. It uses the classes written for the earlier parts of the project (Map, Tower and its subclasses, Critter, CritterGroup, WaveFactory, Player) and the three design patterns of Assignment 3 (targeting Strategy, critter Factory, tower Decorator). It knows nothing about SFML, so the whole game can be tested from the console. The window is drawn by game_main.cpp.

Rules:

  • The player starts with STARTING_COINS coins and STARTING_LIVES lives.
  • Towers are bought and placed on scenery cells, one tower per cell. A tower can be upgraded (up to level 3) or sold for its refund value, which grows with the level and the effects added to the tower.
  • An upgrade wraps the tower in a LevelUpDecorator. The player can also add the effects Splash, Burning and Freezing (one of each per tower), which wrap the tower in a SplashDecorator, BurningDecorator or FreezingDecorator.
  • Each tower has a targeting strategy (nearest to the tower, nearest to the exit, strongest or weakest) that decides which critter in range it shoots. The player can change it at any time (cycleStrategy()).
  • A wave is built by the WaveFactory: the more waves were destroyed, the harder the critters (Normal, Fast, Tank and Boss kinds).
  • startWave() sends the next wave. Critters appear one after the other at the entry and walk along the path to the exit.
  • Each tower shoots at critters within its range, at its rate of fire. A DirectDamageTower hits one critter, an AreaDamageTower hits every critter in range, and a SlowTower hits one critter and slows it. Burning critters take damage every second for a few seconds.
  • Killing a critter gives its reward in coins.
  • A critter that reaches the exit costs the player one life and steals coins equal to its strength.
  • The game is lost when the lives reach 0 and won when the player survives all MAX_WAVES waves.
