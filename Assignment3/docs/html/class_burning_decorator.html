Detailed Description

Burning effect: the damage of a hit is re-applied for a few seconds.

Game rule (from the assignment): "burning: damage to the target critter is re-applied for a few seconds after a hit". Here a hit sets the critter on fire for 3 seconds, and the fire deals a quarter of the tower's power (at least 1) every second. Being hit again restarts the fire; it does not add up.
