Detailed Description

Freezing effect: a hit also slows the critter down for a short time.

Game rule (from the assignment): "freezing: damage to the target critter also slows down the critter". Here a hit freezes the critter for 2 seconds, during which it moves at half speed. Being hit again restarts the 2 seconds, but it never becomes slower than half speed.
